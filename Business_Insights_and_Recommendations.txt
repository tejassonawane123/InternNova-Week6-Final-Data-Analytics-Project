BUSINESS INSIGHTS & RECOMMENDATIONS

1. The majority of orders were successfully delivered, indicating a high overall order completion level in the dataset.

2. The average delivery time was approximately 12.5 days, showing that delivery speed is an important operational metric for monitoring customer experience.

3. The delivery-days distribution is concentrated around lower delivery durations, while a smaller number of orders took considerably longer to deliver.

4. Monthly order activity shows variation over time, with order volumes increasing during several periods and declining during others. 
   This indicates the presence of seasonal or time-based demand patterns.

5. The order-status analysis shows that delivered orders represent the dominant order status, while other statuses such as shipped, canceled, unavailable, 
   invoiced, processing and created represent much smaller proportions.

6. The comparison between estimated and actual delivery days shows variation in delivery performance, indicating that some orders experienced substantial differences 
   between estimated and actual delivery duration.


RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Improve delivery performance by identifying orders and regions associated with longer delivery durations and investigating the operational causes of delays.

2. Use monthly order trends to improve demand planning, logistics capacity and resource allocation during periods of higher order volume.

3. Monitor the gap between estimated and actual delivery times and improve delivery-time estimation where significant differences are observed.

4. Continue monitoring order-status performance and investigate canceled, unavailable and delayed orders to reduce potential customer dissatisfaction.
